# OpsFlow Roadmap

OpsFlow is an evolving operations intelligence project focused on transforming business data into operational visibility, actionable insights, and eventually automated workflows.

The project will be developed incrementally, beginning with a small functional dashboard and increasing in complexity as new technologies and concepts are introduced.

---

## 🏁 Long-Term Vision

OpsFlow should evolve from a simple operational dashboard into a practical operations intelligence system.

The long-term workflow is:

```text
Business Data
      ↓
Data Modeling
      ↓
KPI Calculation
      ↓
Operational Visibility
      ↓
Exception Detection
      ↓
Action
      ↓
Automation
      ↓
Business Intelligence
```

The system should progressively move from **showing what is happening** toward helping identify **what requires attention and what can be automated**.

---

## Guiding Principle

> **Start with the data. Turn it into information. Turn information into action.**

Build the minimum functional version first, then introduce complexity only when it provides a practical benefit.
